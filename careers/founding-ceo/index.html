THE OPENING

Aim higher.
Build what comes next.

Step into the Chief Executive seat of the next-generation Edtech AI market leader. That is the scale of ambition behind this venture.

It requires exceptional commercial discipline, speed, and resilience. The venture establishes a new paradigm in Edtech AI, built to fundamentally transform how people learn and master complex knowledge. The MVP is built, tested, and production-ready.

We bring deep technology and commercial vision together. Having advanced the product through to a completed, validated production MVP, the appointment is open for a London-based Chief Executive Officer to match this technical foundation with commercial execution, go-to-market strategy, and investor engagement alongside the Founder.

The Founder leads continuing technical architecture and product development. The CEO commands the business: customer acquisition, commercial partnerships, operational execution, and the foundations for international scale.

Product architecture and demonstration environments are shared privately with shortlisted candidates during the selection process.

Executive mandate

  • Lead commercial strategy, go-to-market execution, sales operations, and investor meetings within agreed governance.
  • Establish an operating plan with clear commercial priorities, disciplined cash forecasts, and rigorous reporting.
  • Drive enterprise partnerships, customer relationships, and scalable unit economics.
  • Build the operating team as resources permit, with approval for appointments, compensation, and equity commitments.
  • Partner closely with the Founder on product priorities and customer market feedback.

Leadership profile

Candidates demonstrate proven accountability for commercial or operating results in a venture-backed startup, high-growth technology company, or digital-product business.

  • Track record of bringing software products to market, accelerating acquisition, monetization, or operating performance.
  • Disciplined resource management with confidence in budgets, cash flow models, and growth metrics.
  • Decisive communication, intellectual honesty, and the agility to adapt strategy when presented with empirical evidence.
  • Ability to operate from London under an agreed executive working arrangement.

Background in Edtech AI, digital learning, or artificial intelligence products is valuable. Prior CEO title is not a prerequisite; General Managers, Chief Operating Officers, and commercial executives with broad P&L ownership are encouraged to apply.

Working with the Founder

You will hold genuine executive responsibility for daily operations within an agreed strategic plan and delegation. The Founder remains active, retains strategic control, and maintains the authority to redirect company direction, replace the CEO, or step into executive leadership directly, subject to applicable law and governance agreements.

Every company purchase, sale, and investment, including accepting outside capital and committing company funds, requires express prior Founder approval. A budget allocation does not constitute transaction approval. Recurring transaction authority must be explicitly approved with defined scope and limits.

Company sales, IP assignments, acquisitions, mergers, and other reserved matters require Founder consent and corporate approvals. Governance frameworks, executive transition protocols, and earned-equity treatment are fully documented prior to appointment.

Compensation & arrangement

Pre-funding. No cash salary.

No cash salary is provided prior to third-party investor funding or company revenues generating available cash flow. There is no accrued or deferred salary debt, salary advance, or personal founder-funded compensation. Any future cash salary and commencement date require separate board approval and definitive agreement.

The intended equity award is 10% on an agreed fully diluted grant-date basis, subject to finalized capitalization and valid grant documentation. The intended first-hire pool reserve, capitalization schedule, and subsequent financing dilution are fully disclosed prior to acceptance. This is not an immediate share issuance or a guaranteed anti-dilution percentage.

Vesting is structured over four years with a one-year cliff: 25% of the award at month 12, followed by monthly vesting through month 48. Equity represents long-term economic alignment, not guaranteed cash. Complete award mechanics, exercise terms, tax implications, and transfer provisions are provided prior to formal agreement.

Time commitment, working pattern, commencement date, and a legally supportable engagement structure consistent with these terms must be finalized before work begins. The title does not itself create employment status or promise a statutory director appointment.
